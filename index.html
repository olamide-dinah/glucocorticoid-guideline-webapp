Based on: Bacher C et al., "International Consensus Guidance for the Management of Glucocorticoid Related Complications in Neuromuscular Disease." Muscle & Nerve 2025;71:309–316. This tool is a clinical decision aid only. Always apply professional judgement, consider local protocols, and involve the relevant multidisciplinary team. Guidance on infectious risk (e.g., TB, Strongyloides, PJP, hepatitis, HIV, meningococcus) varies by region and patient population — always check local and regional public health guidelines in addition to this tool.
